Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about admissions and studying at Green Up Academy

Apply for a visa through the French consulate in your country. Check the consulate's website for specific procedures and processing times, which may take several months. Required documents vary by country and visa type. Note: Your passport must be valid for the entire duration of your stay in France, or your application will be rejected.

Visit Campus France for student visa procedures, which vary by country:

  • Procedure for 33 countries with online registration (CEF)
  • Procedure for European countries
  • Procedure for other countries

When submitting your application, there are two possibilities:

If Green Up Academy is listed in your country: Select our institution, follow the steps, and provide the visa pack issued by Green Up Academy upon enrollment.

If Green Up Academy is not listed: Enter "Green Up Academy" in the text field and attach the visa pack provided by the school.

Campus France will verify your enrollment with our services.

Admissions are open for October 2025 and February 2026 sessions. The application deadline is one month before the start date. Key dates:

  • 15 October 2025
  • 10 February 2026
